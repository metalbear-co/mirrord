Added `mirrord exec --share` and the `share` option, which print a temporary HTTPS link to the service running on your machine, through the share-ingress of a Team or Enterprise operator. A shared session steals only the requests that carry its session key in `baggage`, or that match its `http_filter`. Sharing needs permission to get `sessions` in the `operator.metalbear.co` API group, which the operator's chart grants to mirrord users.

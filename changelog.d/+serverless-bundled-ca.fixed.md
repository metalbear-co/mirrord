The serverless workload companion and the CLI's sessions-manager connection trust the CA certificates bundled into mirrord, alongside the system's when it has any, so application images no longer need `ca-certificates` installed to connect to MetalBear Cloud. The data-plane connection trusts the system's CA certificates too, so a sessions-manager behind a private CA or a TLS-inspecting proxy no longer passes registration only to fail every data-plane connection.

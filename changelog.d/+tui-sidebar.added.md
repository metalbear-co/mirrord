The TUI has a sidebar listing every screen, grouped into start, watch and setup with live counts, the current cluster and the connection state; click a screen to go there. `Shift+Tab` goes back a screen without getting stuck in the Terminal, lists take mouse clicks and the wheel, and text boxes support `Ctrl+A`, `Shift`-arrow selection and mouse selection. Colors carry one meaning everywhere: mint for ready, amber for a guess to check, coral for failed or risky.

The Windows layer now bypasses its own hooks per call, not per thread. `layer-win` gained `hooks::reentrancy`, the port of the unix layer's `DETOUR_BYPASS` and `#[hook_guard_fn]`, and `#[internal_bypass]` installs it for every annotated detour, so a nested hooked call that a detour body makes reaches the original function instead of coming back into the layer. A detour body leaves the guard while it calls back into application code, which `addrinfo_ex::deliver` does. The `FreeAddrInfoW`, `FreeAddrInfoExW` and `GetAddrInfoExCancel` hooks lost their `#[internal_bypass]`, because each decides by the pointer or the synthetic handle it is given rather than by who called it; for the two free hooks a bypass gave a layer-allocated chain to `ws2_32`, which is the `0xC0000374` double free that the thread-wide marker caused on the task-pool workers.

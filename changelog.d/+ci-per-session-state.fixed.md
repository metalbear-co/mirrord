Fixed concurrent `mirrord ci start` and `mirrord ci container` sessions waiting on each other and on `mirrord ci stop`, and failing when that wait took longer than 5 seconds. Each session now keeps its state in its own directory, `mirrord-ci/<id>/` in the temporary directory, and `mirrord ci stop` skips a session that another mirrord process is using and warns about it instead of failing. `mirrord ci stop` doesn't stop sessions that an older mirrord version started.

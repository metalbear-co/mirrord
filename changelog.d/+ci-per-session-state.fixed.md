Fixed concurrent `mirrord ci start` and `mirrord ci container` sessions waiting on each other, and on a `mirrord ci stop` that was stopping another session, and failing when that wait took longer than 5 seconds. Each session now keeps its state in its own directory, `mirrord-ci/<id>/` in the temporary directory, and `mirrord ci stop` skips a session that another mirrord process is using and warns about it instead of failing. When `mirrord ci stop` can't stop something, its error now lists each process, process group and container that it left running. `mirrord ci stop` doesn't stop sessions that an older mirrord version started.
